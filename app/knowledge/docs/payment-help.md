# Failed or pending payments
If a payment fails, you can usually retry after confirming that your bank has not debited the amount. If money was debited but the portal still shows payment pending or does not show a confirmation, do not pay again immediately. Keep the transaction reference and a screenshot, wait for the bank/gateway to settle, then contact the official admissions or payment support channel so they can verify the transaction.

# Staying safe
The university and this assistant will never ask for your card PIN, CVV, OTP or password. Do not share them with anyone.
